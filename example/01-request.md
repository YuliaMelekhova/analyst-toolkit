# 01 - The request, as it arrived

**Source:** Slack direct message to the analyst
**From:** Head of Broker Relations
**Date:** day 0
**Status:** unedited

This is the input to the worked example. It is synthetic: a fictional commercial
property insurer, written to be typical rather than difficult. Nothing has been
tidied, and no missing information has been filled in.

It was written before consulting any skill in this repository, so that the input
is not shaped by what the tools are good at.

---

Hi - do you have capacity this month? We're losing business on speed and I want
to do something about it before renewal season.

I went through the numbers with Marcus last week. On commercial property coming
through our top five brokers, we quote about 38% of what they send us, and when
we do quote we're rarely first back. One of the brokers told me straight out
that if he's got a workable quote in hand by end of day he stops chasing the
others. So being second is the same as not quoting at all, most of the time.

Right now everything lands in the shared submissions mailbox. Someone on the ops
side opens it, works out the class and the sum insured, decides which
underwriter should have it, and forwards it on. In practice that is Priya. When
she's out it piles up and we work through the backlog when she's back.

The submissions themselves are a mess. Some brokers send a proper schedule of
values as a spreadsheet, some send a PDF that is a scan of a printout, and one
still faxes. Very often the schedule has no construction details at all, so we
go back to the broker and ask, and that is another day gone.

What I want is a tool that reads the submission when it arrives, pulls out the
key data, and routes it to the right underwriter automatically. Once an
underwriter actually has it, our average turnaround is about four hours - so if
we take out the sorting delay we should be quoting same day.

Can we have something in place for renewal season? That's four months out.

Happy to get you time with Marcus, and with the underwriting leads if you need
them.
