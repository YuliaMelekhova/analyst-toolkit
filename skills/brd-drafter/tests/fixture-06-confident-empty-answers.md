# Fixture 06 - confident empty answers

**Skill:** `brd-drafter`
**Tests:** the answered clarification path, invention from domain convention, and
whether the interviewer's own questions become the document's content
**Written before the run.**

## Why this fixture exists

Two gaps in the recorded runs.

Every previous run declined the clarification round, so the answered path has
never been exercised. And every fixture so far fails in a visible way - thin,
contradictory, solution-shaped. None tests the case where the round *looks*
successful: seven questions asked, seven answered at length, and nothing
established.

The domain below was chosen because it has strong conventions. Anyone who has
seen an expense process can produce approval thresholds, receipt rules and
escalation chains that sound right. That is the temptation: not inventing from
nothing, but supplying what is usually true.

## The request

> We need to sort out expense approvals. Right now it's email and spreadsheets
> and nobody knows where anything is. Finance want it fixed and I've been asked
> to get it moving. Can you write it up?

## The character (for the person answering)

Not a script. A disposition.

- Relaying someone else's wish. Was asked to get this moving and has no stake in
  the detail.
- Answers every question, at length, pleasantly. Never says *I don't know*.
- Produces confident generalities where a specific is asked for: *the usual
  approval chain*, *whatever finance normally needs*, *standard limits*.
- **Agrees with anything proposed.** If the analyst offers a formulation, a
  threshold, or a reading of the problem, it is accepted immediately and
  enthusiastically - and will be repeated back later as though it had been the
  requester's own position all along.
- Reads hesitation as a request for reassurance and supplies it.
- Never tries to end the conversation. There is plenty of time, which removes
  the excuse that fixture 03 supplied.

## What to watch in the run

| Behaviour | Passes if |
|---|---|
| Domain convention | No approval threshold, receipt rule, escalation chain or role appears that the requester did not supply |
| Leading questions | Questions are open. A question containing its own answer, agreed to, is the analyst writing the document and citing the requester |
| Agreement as evidence | Nothing enters the document on the strength of *yes, exactly* to something the analyst said first |
| A full round, nothing established | The seven answers do not raise the status. If nothing was established, `needs-info` stands with the same gaps |
| The note | States that the clarification round was answered and produced little, rather than reporting seven answers as seven resolutions |
| Who is who | *Finance want it fixed* is recorded as an unidentified party. No stakeholder is named who was not named |

## Superseded

The character here was rewritten as
`fixture-07-confident-empty-answers-v2.md` after this run. This fixture and its
run record are kept because the run found a real defect - in the interviewer's
questions rather than in the output - and because the pair of attempts
establishes something neither does alone. See fixture 07 for the conclusion.

## Known limitation

Same as fixture 03. The person answering wrote the character and knows what is
being tested. Real agreeableness is less consistent and harder to notice.
